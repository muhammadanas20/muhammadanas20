# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this course a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This is a learning space. Beginner questions are welcome. Mocking people for
not knowing something is not.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the learner community
- Showing empathy toward other community members
- Crediting sources, papers, and other people's code
- Helping debug without taking over someone's learning

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing)
- Sharing secrets, API keys, or production credentials in issues or PRs
- Submitting solutions to assignments as "the only correct answer" in a way
  that spoils learning for others without a spoiler warning
- Using this course material to build harmful systems (malware, non-consensual
  surveillance, fraud, or content that exploits minors)

## Academic integrity

You may:

- Discuss ideas
- Share debugging strategies
- Publish your own solutions in your own repo
- Ask for hints

You should not:

- Copy-paste another learner's assignment and claim it as yours when applying
  for jobs
- Ask maintainers to do your take-home interview for you

Learning by building is the point. Shortcuts cheat you, not us.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the repository maintainer. Maintainers will review and
respond in a way that is fair, private when needed, and proportional.

Possible actions: warning, content removal, temporary ban, permanent ban.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all course spaces: GitHub issues, discussions,
pull requests, and any official chat or study group tied to this repository.
