# FAQ — Phase 8: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

### LangChain or raw?

Raw first (this phase's code/). Then a framework if the graph grows. Interviews expect you to explain without the framework.

### How big a gold set?

25 is a start. 50–100 is better. Quality of labels > thousands of noisy ones.

### Can I use the LLM to generate eval questions?

As a draft. Humans must edit. Models reuse phrasing from the corpus and overfit retrieval.
