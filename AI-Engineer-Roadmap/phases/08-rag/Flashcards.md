# Flashcards — Phase 8: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Say the answer. Then open. Do the deck two days later. That is the whole spaced-repetition system.

**Q1.** Naive RAG?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Retrieve top-k, stuff prompt, generate.

</details>

**Q2.** Hybrid?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Keyword + dense, fused.

</details>

**Q3.** Rerank?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Rescore candidates with a stronger model.

</details>

**Q4.** Faithfulness?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Answer supported by retrieved text.

</details>

**Q5.** CRAG?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Grade retrieval, correct if poor.

</details>

**Q6.** Self-RAG?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Retrieve/critique on demand.

</details>

**Q7.** GraphRAG?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Retrieve via a knowledge graph.

</details>

**Q8.** Why citations?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

User trust + you can debug.

</details>

**Q9.** Query rewrite?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Standalone search query from chatty history.

</details>

**Q10.** When not RAG?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No corpus, or three paragraphs, or SQL is the answer.

</details>
