# Flashcards — Phase 10: Model Context Protocol (MCP)

Say the answer. Then open. Do the deck two days later. That is the whole spaced-repetition system.

**Q1.** MCP stands for?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Model Context Protocol.

</details>

**Q2.** Three primitives?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Tools, resources, prompts.

</details>

**Q3.** stdio vs HTTP?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

stdout is JSON-RPC.

</details>

**Q5.** USB analogy?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Standard plug for model capabilities.

</details>

**Q6.** Auth on remote?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Required. Tokens/OAuth/mTLS.

</details>

**Q7.** Least privilege?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Fewest tools and narrowest filesystem roots.

</details>

**Q8.** Is MCP the model?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

No.

</details>

**Q9.** IDE risk?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Model reads secrets in the repo.

</details>

**Q10.** When not MCP?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Single internal function, one caller.

</details>
